## What is Vi?

**Vi Project Management** ([useviapp.com](https://useviapp.com)) connects the whole path of a piece of work in one product:

1. A customer reports a bug or asks for a feature, by **email** or through a **client portal**.
2. The request becomes a **task**, and the team moves it through its **own workflow**, with approvals and an SLA per step.
3. The developer finishes the work **from the IDE** with **Commit & Close**.
4. The customer gets an **automatic update** with a link to the request's status.

We call this **Close the Loop**: the developer closes the task, and Vi closes the loop with the customer.

Vi is built for:
- **Software product teams** that receive bug reports and feature requests from their users.
- **Agencies and software service companies** that build and maintain software for clients.
- **Development teams** that want task tracking connected to their IDE and Git, without changing how they write code.

## Commit & Close

Developers see their tasks inside the IDE, start one, and finish it with a single action:

- All of the task's source changes go into **one commit tagged with the task ID**, and the commit is pushed.
- **Vi then moves the task to the next step** of the workflow.
- The reviewer or QA in the next step sees **exactly the files changed for that task**.
- If the push fails, the task does not move.

## Helpdesk and customer requests

- **Shared email inbox**: customer emails become requests with a full conversation thread.
- **Client portal**: one request form per project, built with a drag-and-drop form builder and custom fields. Customers choose *bug* or *feature request*.
- **Talk before you convert**: reply to the customer on the request itself, then turn it into a task when it is ready.
- **Automatic customer updates** with a status page link when the work is done.
- **Live customer chat**, canned responses, triage and **CSAT** surveys.
- Customer messages can require approval before they are sent.
- Customers and requesters **do not take paid seats**.

## Tasks, workflows and teams

- A workflow per team, with **approvals** and an **SLA per step**.
- Alerts for overdue tasks, tasks without updates and SLA breaches.
- Work passes between teams (support → development → QA) while each team keeps its own workflow and intake form.
- Subtasks and checklists, **dependencies**, milestones, epics, **releases and versions**, templates and custom fields.
- Four priority levels including Critical, Markdown descriptions, attachments, comments and @mentions.
- A full history of every step each task went through, who moved it and how long it stayed.
- Built-in **time tracking**, estimates and progress.
- **Flow-based by design**: work moves across a Kanban board continuously, without sprints, and each person has one active task at a time.

## Views and reports
